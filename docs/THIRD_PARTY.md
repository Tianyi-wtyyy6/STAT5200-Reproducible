(commit 58eda583ea28645a3531cd769b7852ff504656df), which declares MIT licensing.
The upstream license and copyright record are retained in `docs/licenses/`.

The uploaded `research/original/sources/Synth*.R` files are third-party API/source
snapshots for provenance, not altered implementations. Synth declares GPL >= 2;
a copy of GPL-2 is included. Its upstream source is https://github.com/j-hai/Synth .
Package citations are retained in `research/original/metadata/software_citations/`.
Installed dependencies retain their own licenses. No blanket license is asserted
for the author's research code or for unrelated manuscript materials.
